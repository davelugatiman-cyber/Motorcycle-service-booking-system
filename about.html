MotoCare — Motorcycle Service Booking System

Your Ride, Our Care. A prototype website that lets motorcycle owners see service prices, work out a rough cost and request a workshop appointment without phoning ahead.

See Services Request an appointment

Project overview

Why this project exists

The build-up from the problem we found, to the gap in what riders have today, to what this website proposes.

01

The problem

Riders in the district keep their motorcycles running on guesswork. Servicing is skipped to save money, small faults are ignored until a bike breaks down mid-week, and the cost of an unplanned repair is always higher than the cost of a planned service.

  • Service history is easy to lose
  • Faults are noticed too late
  • Repairs are paid for at the worst time
02

The gap in current solutions

Local workshops still take bookings by phone or a queue at the counter. Riders cannot see prices before travelling, cannot tell how long a job takes, and cannot request a slot without taking time off work to call.

  • No service list or prices online
  • Bookings depend on phone calls
  • No way to plan or compare a cost
03

The proposed solution

A single website where a rider can read what each service includes, calculate a rough cost with the estimator and send an appointment request with a preferred date and time.

  • Service catalogue with sample prices
  • A cost estimator for a quick budget
  • An appointment request form
1 Selected local community for this prototype: Riverside, Metro City

Target users

Motorcycle owners in one local community

This prototype is designed for a single, specific group rather than "every rider" everywhere.

  • Community: Riverside, Metro City

    A dense residential district where most households own one motorcycle, and where the nearest garage is a fifteen minute ride away. Riders here are the primary users of this prototype.

  • Daily commuters and delivery riders

    People who ride every day, cannot afford long breakdowns, and need a service slot that fits around a work schedule.

  • First and second bike owners

    Riders who are not confident about what a service should cost and want a written record of the work before they pay for it.

  • Secondary user: the workshop team

    The same site gives the shop a structured list of requests, so time is spent on bikes instead of on answering the same phone call all day.

Expected benefits

What changes if this works

The prototype is expected to help riders and the workshop in different but connected ways.

For the rider

  • Service prices are visible before travelling to the workshop
  • A rough cost can be calculated in seconds with the estimator
  • An appointment can be requested without a phone call
  • Fewer skipped services, so fewer roadside breakdowns

For the workshop

  • Bookings arrive as structured requests with the details already filled in
  • Fewer phone calls and less counter time spent quoting
  • A clearer view of which services are in demand
  • More services booked in advance instead of walk-ins

This is an IT105 student project prototype.

MotoCare — Motorcycle Service Booking System is a student prototype built for an academic web development course. It is not a live business, it is not affiliated with any real workshop, and it does not process payments. The prices are sample figures and the contact details are placeholders to be verified with a real business. Forms demonstrate validation and submission only: nothing is sent to a server, and no appointment is booked until a workshop confirms it with the rider.
