Questions

About these prices

Are these prices real?

No. They are sample prices prepared for this student project. They have not been verified with a real motorcycle shop and should be updated once you have researched a local business. The workshop always confirms the real price in writing before work begins.

What is the difference between an estimate and a quotation?

An estimate uses the published sample prices, so it is a planning tool. A quotation is prepared after the motorcycle has been inspected and shows the exact work and cost. You only pay the amount on the approved quotation.

Why can I enter more than one job?

Because the same service is often booked several times over, for example a chain adjustment on two bikes, or an oil change for a whole family. The estimator simply multiplies the sample price by the number of jobs.

Do you service all motorcycle brands?

We service Japanese, Indian, European and American brands, including retro bikes. For rare or imported models we order the correct parts and keep you updated on the timeline.

Happy with the estimate?

Send an appointment request with your preferred date. Our team will call you to confirm the slot and the real price.
